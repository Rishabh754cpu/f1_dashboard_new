Transforming F1 Data Into Championship Intelligence

This project takes Formula 1 race results, qualifying sessions, lap telemetry and championship standings and turns them into an interactive analytics platform built in Tableau — made for fans, analysts and strategists.

From pole-position performance to championship trends, the dashboard covers every dimension of modern Formula 1 racing, and the story walks through the key findings step by step.

  • Global Race OverviewCircuit-by-circuit analysis across all Grand Prix locations with performance comparisons.
  • Driver Performance MetricsWins, podiums, poles, DNFs and consistency analysis for every driver.
  • Constructor Championship BattleTeam points, trends and title history across seasons.
